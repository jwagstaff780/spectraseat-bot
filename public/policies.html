Store policies

Review and adapt these to your jurisdiction before launch — they are a sensible starting template, not legal advice.

Shipping

Every order ships free with tracking. Orders are processed within 1–3 business days, then typically delivered within the window shown on each product page. We email your tracking number as soon as it's issued. If we can't ship within the time we quoted, we'll email you and you can cancel for a full refund.

Returns & refunds

You can request a return within 30 days of delivery. Email with your order number. If an item arrives damaged or not as described, we'll replace it or refund you in full — no return needed. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval.

Privacy

We collect only what's needed to fulfil your order: your name, email, phone and shipping address. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details. Your shipping details are shared with our fulfilment partner solely to deliver your order. We don't sell your data. Email to request a copy or deletion of your data.

Contact

Email — we reply within one business day.
