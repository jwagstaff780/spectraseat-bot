Store policies

Review and adapt these to your jurisdiction before launch — they are a sensible starting template, not legal advice.

Shipping

Every order ships free with tracking. Orders are processed within 1–3 business days, then typically delivered within the window shown on each product page. We email your tracking number as soon as it's issued. If we can't ship within the time we quoted, we'll email you and you can cancel for a full refund. We deliver within the UK. Items ship from our fulfilment partners' warehouses in the UK, Europe or overseas; prices include any VAT due, and there's nothing extra to pay on delivery for orders under £135.

Returns & refunds

Supplements and food: for health and hygiene reasons, sealed products can only be returned if the seal is unbroken; once opened they can't be returned unless faulty or not as described. This is an exception the Consumer Contracts Regulations allow.

You have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of delivery (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013), and we go further: You can request a return within 30 days of delivery. Email with your order number. If an item arrives damaged or not as described, we'll replace it or refund you in full — no return needed. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval.

Privacy

We collect only what's needed to fulfil your order: your name, email, phone and delivery address. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details. Your delivery details are shared with our fulfilment partner solely to deliver your order. We don't sell your data.

Cookies and similar storage. Your basket uses strictly necessary storage in your browser. Only if you click "Accept" in our cookie banner do we remember which advert brought you here and share a record of your purchase (with your email address in hashed, unreadable form) with Meta to measure and improve our ads. You can change your choice at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Your rights. Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it: email . If you're unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
