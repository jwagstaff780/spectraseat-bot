EDGE FINDER

How each card works

We compare a trusted bookmaker's price (the "sharp price") against the best price you can actually get elsewhere. If the price you can get is better than what the trusted bookmaker thinks is fair, that's an edge. We show three ways of estimating "fair" side by side — the middle one ("Best estimate") is the one we actually use.

Bet size settings

0.25 = quarter-size bets (safer, recommended). 1.0 = full-size bets (much riskier). The second box is a hard ceiling — we'll never suggest betting more than that percentage of your bankroll on one bet, no matter what the maths says.
