Frequently asked questions

Orders & delivery

How long will my order take?

Orders are dispatched within 1–3 business days. Each product page shows an estimated delivery window for that item, based on where it ships from. You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as it ships.

Where do you ship from?

From our fulfilment partners' warehouses: some items ship from the UK or Europe and some from overseas, which is why delivery windows differ between products. Delivery is always free and tracked.

Where's my order?

Use Track your order with your email and order number, or ask the Help chat. Tracking can take 2–3 days to show movement after it's first issued.

What if my order is late?

If your order hasn't shipped within the expected time, we'll email you, and you can cancel for a full refund. If it's well past its delivery window, the Help chat can refund you straight away.

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver across the United Kingdom. Prices include any VAT due, and for orders under £135 you won't be asked to pay anything extra on delivery.

Can I change my address or cancel?

Contact us as soon as possible through the chat or contact form. We can usually update it before the order ships.

Returns & refunds

What's your returns policy?

30 days from delivery — longer than the 14-day cancellation right UK law gives you. Items should be unused and in original packaging. See the full returns policy.

My item arrived damaged or wrong.

Sorry about that! Send us a photo via chat or the contact form and we'll send a replacement or refund you in full. You don't need to send it back.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds go back to your original payment method and usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Payments & pricing

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major cards, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay where your device supports them.

How does multi-buy pricing work?

On selected items, buying 2 saves 10% and 3 or more saves 15%. You can mix options (like colours) of the same product. The discount is applied automatically in your cart.

Do you have discount codes?

When we run promotions, you can enter the code on the checkout page.

Reviews

Are your reviews real?

Our star rating comes only from verified buyers, who get a review link after delivery. We publish reviews as written, good or bad. Some products also show reviews from buyers of the same item on another marketplace; these are clearly labelled and not counted in our rating.
