About

We’re a small UK online store with one job: find the genuinely useful products people are talking about, check them properly, and sell them at a fair price with delivery you can rely on.

How we choose products

Most products we look at never make it to the shop. Before anything is listed we check that it's good value, ships well, isn't a knock-off of someone else's brand, and has clear, honest photos. We keep checking stock and prices after it's listed, and we pull products that start getting complaints.

How we handle your order

Orders are packed and shipped by our fulfilment partners, some in the UK and Europe and some overseas. That's how we keep prices down. Each product page shows a realistic delivery window for that item, you get tracking as soon as it ships, and if anything is delayed we tell you and offer a full refund.

Our promises

  • Free tracked shipping on every order.
  • 30-day returns. Damaged or not as described? We replace or refund it, no return needed.
  • Honest reviews: our rating comes only from verified buyers, and we never hide negative reviews.
  • Real answers from our support team, 7 days a week.
